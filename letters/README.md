# Octo's ABCs

A letters and letter-sounds game for a 4–5 year old getting ready for kindergarten. It runs in Safari on an iPad or phone, with nothing to install.

**Link:** https://applesandbeer.github.io/Octopus/letters/ (open it in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It shows up as "Octo ABCs".)

## How it teaches

What research and kindergarten-readiness checklists agree on for this age:

1. **Letter sounds matter as much as letter names.** Kids learn best when names, sounds and a picture keyword go together: "S says /s/, like sun." Studies find that letter-sound teaching works best alongside sound games like rhyming and beginning sounds.
2. **A sound-friendly order, not A to Z.** Many phonics programs start with **s a t p i n**, which have common, easy-to-hear sounds. Then come m d g o c k e u r h b f l… with **b and d far apart**, since they're easy to mix up.
3. **Her name first.** Letters in a child's own name are the easiest to learn and the most motivating, so they jump to the front of the line.
4. **Big and little letters.** Kindergarten expects both, so matching uppercase to lowercase has its own game.
5. **Hear the first sound.** Matching a picture to its first sound (sun → s) is a core readiness skill.
6. **Rhyming.** Spotting rhymes (cat / hat) is one of the earliest sound skills on readiness checklists.
7. **Hands-on.** Tracing a letter with a finger helps her remember its shape.
8. **Short, playful and kind.** Rounds are 5 questions. A wrong answer just fades out and Octo names what she picked ("That's D"). After two misses, the right answer glows, so she always ends on a win.

## The six games

| Game | What she does | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| 📖 **ABC Book** | Flip through A–Z. Tap the letter or pictures to hear them. | Letter names, sounds, keywords |
| 🖼️ **Picture Match** | See a letter and tap the picture that starts with its sound. Later: see a picture and tap its first letter. | Beginning sounds |
| 🎈 **Balloon Pop** | Pop the floating balloons with the right letter on them | Letter recognition (see it → hear the name → hear just the sound) |
| 🍪 **Feed Munch** | Feed the monster the little letter that matches his BIG letter, and the reverse | Uppercase ↔ lowercase, look-alikes (b d p q) |
| 🖌️ **Magic Paint** | Trace big and little letters with a finger in rainbow paint | Letter shapes |
| 🎵 **Rhyme Time** | "What rhymes with cat?" Pick the picture. | Rhyming |

**It grows with her.** She starts with 6 letters. When she gets each one right 3 of her last 6 tries, 3 new letters unlock with a celebration. Each game also has 4 levels that go up after a good round and down after a hard one. The **🔤 My Letters** page turns letters gold as she learns them. She wins a sticker every round (📒).

## Grown-ups

On the home screen, **press and hold "Grown-ups"** for one second. There you can:
- Enter her **name**. Octo greets her, and her name's letters are taught first.
- Choose **"add letters as she learns"** (best) or **all 26 letters now**.
- Change game levels, and turn the voice on or off.
- See **what she knows**: first-try right answers for each letter's name and sound.
- **Record your own letter sounds** (optional, recommended). Phone voices can't say a clean "/b/". They say "buh" or worse. Tap 🎙 next to a letter and say just the sound, short and clipped. The game then uses your voice for that sound. Recordings stay on the device.

### Tips for home
- One or two games a day (5–10 minutes) beats one long session.
- Say sounds short with no "uh" on the end: /b/, not "buh". Stretch the sounds that can stretch: "mmm", "sss".
- Play "I Spy" with sounds: "I spy something that starts with /s/."
- Point out letters in her name on signs, boxes and books. Make letters from play-dough or draw them in sand.

## Technical notes

- Everything is in `index.html`: plain HTML, CSS and older-style (ES5) JavaScript, with no outside downloads. It uses the device's built-in voice.
- Pictures are built-in emoji, plus a hand-drawn igloo.
- Progress and recordings are saved only on that device.
