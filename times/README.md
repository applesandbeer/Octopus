# Octo's Times Tables

A multiplication practice game for a 1st grader. It runs in Safari on an iPad, with nothing to install.

**Link:** https://applesandbeer.github.io/Octopus/times/ (open it in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It shows up as "Octo Times".)

## How it teaches

Research and teaching guides agree on a few things for young kids:

1. **Meaning first, memorizing last.** Kids learn best by going from **real objects → pictures → symbols** (called "concrete–representational–abstract"). A 1st grader should first understand that 3 × 2 means *3 groups of 2* before memorizing anything.
2. **Equal groups, then arrays.** "Groups of" is the easiest idea to grasp. Rows and columns (arrays) come next. They also show that 3 × 4 and 4 × 3 are the same, so there are half as many facts to learn.
3. **Skip counting is the bridge.** Counting by 2s, 5s and 10s is how kids first work out products. It's also a 1st-grade skill already.
4. **Learn the easy facts first, then build from them.** Start with 2s, 10s and 5s, then 1s. Later facts come from known ones (6 × 4 is 5 × 4 plus one more 4).
5. **Short, frequent practice with no time pressure.** Whether timed tests cause math anxiety is debated, but kids can learn facts well without them. There's no timer here, only stars.
6. **Spaced repetition.** Facts he misses come back more often. Facts he knows come back less.

## The four games (play in order)

| # | Game | What he does | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🍪 **Snack Plates** | Puts the same number of snacks on each plate for Octo, then counts them all by 2s, 3s… | Equal groups. "3 groups of 2" |
| 2 | 🌻 **Garden Rows** | Taps each row of flowers to count them, then turns the garden sideways | Arrays and turn-around facts (3 × 4 = 4 × 3) |
| 3 | 🐸 **Frog Hops** | Taps where the frog lands when it hops by 2s, 5s or 10s on a 1–50 chart | Skip counting = multiplying |
| 4 | ⭐ **Times Facts** | Answers 3 × 2 = ? with a "Show me" button that draws the groups | The × sign, starting with the easiest facts |

Every right answer shows both forms: **3 groups of 2 = 6** and **3 × 2 = 6**.

**Fun parts:** Octo the octopus reads everything aloud (tap Octo to hear it again). He earns a star for each first-try answer and a sticker for every round of 5. Stickers fill his sticker book.

**It adjusts to him:** each game has 5 levels. A round with 4–5 first-try answers moves him up a level. A hard round moves him down.

## Grown-ups

On the home screen, **press and hold "Grown-ups"** for one second. You can turn the voice on or off, change levels, and see a chart of which times facts he knows.

Tips:
- Ask "How did you know?" Counting by 2s or 5s is a great answer.
- Use real things: socks in pairs, egg cartons in rows, nickels in 5s.
- One or two rounds a day beats one long session.

## Technical notes

- Everything is in `index.html`: plain HTML, CSS and older-style (ES5) JavaScript, so it runs on older iPads. There are no outside downloads.
- Progress is saved only on that iPad.
